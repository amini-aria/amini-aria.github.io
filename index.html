Bio

I split my time between the field and the terminal. As a geologist I map rock formations, edit reference books for the Iranian earth-science community, and serve the Iranian Geological Society. As a software entrepreneur, I run Varjavand — a company building tools for researchers — and manage the SARMAD high-performance computing infrastructure at Shahid Beheshti University.
