Bio

I am Mohammad Aria Amini, a Geology graduate and an active researcher and science communicator in the field of Earth sciences. My academic and professional journey began with an interest in understanding the Earth and the processes that shape it, and has gradually expanded into research, scientific writing and editing, and the application of modern technologies to Earth sciences.

My research interests cover various areas of Earth sciences, with a particular interest in geochemistry, petroleum geology, and subsurface resources. Alongside academic study and research, I am interested in using modern approaches to data processing and analysis to explore and address scientific problems.

Another part of my work focuses on scientific writing and editing. I have contributed to the writing and editing of books and specialized publications in Earth sciences, while also developing an interest in the history and evolution of geological knowledge.

I am also interested in technology and its applications in scientific research. Programming, data processing, computational tools, and emerging technologies are areas I pursue as a means of developing research methods and building connections between Earth sciences and technology.

My goal is to continue learning and conducting research while building meaningful connections between geology, data, and technology, and to contribute to both scientific knowledge and the creation and dissemination of valuable scientific work.
